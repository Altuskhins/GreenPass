# GreenPass 2.6.5

- AWG self-healing. Once a minute GreenPass checks that Telegram is reachable through the AWG tunnel. After 3 failed checks in a row it creates a new Cloudflare WARP config for a free config, or reconnects a user config. At most once per 10 minutes.
- AWG: the keepalive defaults to 25 s, so an idle tunnel is no longer dropped by NAT.
- AWG: with an IPv4-only config, IPv6 targets are refused at once instead of failing with "missing IPv6 local address".
